Features Requiring an Online Connection

While offline, you can view your downloaded wallets, categories, and transactions, queue new income or expense entries, and queue edits or deletions with revision tracking. The following actions require a live server connection:

  • Creating or modifying wallets, categories, category budgets, or savings goals
  • Recording wallet-to-wallet transfers or savings contributions
  • Creating shared spaces, generating or accepting partner invitations, and changing membership
  • Refreshing external exchange-rate providers or saving base-currency preferences
  • Exporting full server CSV/JSON archives, changing passwords, or deleting accounts
